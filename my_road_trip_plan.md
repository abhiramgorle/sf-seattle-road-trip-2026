## IMPORTANT NOTES

- **Fern Canyon road:** Confirm with rental company that unpaved road + stream crossings are permitted. Do not assume.
- **Waterfalls in September:** Yosemite and most Sierra falls are at low to minimal flow. Manage expectations.
- **Fires:** Grand Park 2 Fire has closed Sunrise and White River at Rainier (plan uses Paradise). Mt. Hood area has active closures. Recheck both 48–72 hrs before respective days.
- **Crater Lake:** East Rim closed, Cleetwood Cove closed all 2026. West Rim + Watchman are excellent.
- **Labor Day (Sep 7):** Rainier and I-5 will be extremely busy. Pre-dawn start is not optional, it's required.
- **Fuel:** Never below half-tank near Yosemite, Redwoods, Crater Lake, or Rainier. Download offline maps — cell service is unreliable throughout.
- **Cold nights:** September mornings in the Redwoods and Cascades can be 40–50°F. Pack warm layers even if Yosemite afternoons hit 90°F.
