Day-by-Day Plan
Day 1 — Wednesday, September 2
San Francisco → Yosemite → Knights Ferry
~285 miles | ~6.5–7 hrs driving | Overnight: Rivers Edge RV Park, Knights Ferry
September Yosemite note: Waterfalls are at their lowest in early September. Yosemite Falls is typically dry; Vernal Fall is a trickle. This is fine — the valley granite, meadows, and El Capitan views are spectacular year-round. Don't build the itinerary around waterfalls.
TimeStopDurationNotes
04:30Depart San Francisco—Fill gas and prep breakfast/lunch the night before.
08:45Tunnel View + Bridalveil Fall viewpoint45mClassic El Capitan–Half Dome panorama. Minimal water at Bridalveil in September but iconic framing.
09:45Park once in Yosemite Valley—Use the free valley shuttle; avoid moving the car repeatedly.
10:00Cook's Meadow loop1hBest granite wall and El Cap views from the meadow; easy walk.
11:15Lower Yosemite Fall loop30mWorth a look even if mostly dry.
12:00Picnic lunch beside the Merced River45mUse bear boxes; never leave food unattended.
13:00Mist Trail to Vernal Fall Footbridge1.5hModerate hike. Turn around at the footbridge — going further adds time and the falls are low.
14:45El Capitan Meadow + Valley View45mFinal photo stops on the way out.
15:45Drive to Knights Ferry~2hExit via CA-120 W (Big Oak Flat Road). Download route offline before entering the park.
18:00Arrive Knights Ferry—Check in, rest.
Food
  • Pack breakfast and picnic from SF the night before
  • Yosemite Village Store — groceries and sandwiches if needed
  • Degnan's Kitchen — quick meal only if the line is short

Many rental agreements explicitly prohibit this and it can void insurance coverage. Confirm in writing with the rental company before booking the Fern Canyon permit. If not permitted, substitute the Prairie Creek Foothill Trail + Big Tree loop — no permit required.

Day 6 — Monday, September 7 (Labor Day)
Mt. Rainier Paradise → Seattle
~195 miles | ~4–4.5 hrs driving (+1–1.5h Labor Day traffic) | End: Seattle
Labor Day warning: This is the single busiest day of the year at Mt. Rainier. Paradise parking fills by 8–9 AM. Reaching the Nisqually Entrance before 7:00 AM is not optional — it's necessary. I-5 into Seattle is heavy from 2 PM onward. Sunrise and White River are closed (Grand Park 2 Fire) — Paradise is the correct choice.

Total post-rental: ~$1,600 / ~$800 per person. All-in with rental share: ~$950/person.

Nonresident warning: If neither traveler qualifies for the $80 resident America the Beautiful Pass, the $250 nonresident pass adds $170 to the total. Offset by using tent backup sites for two nights.

Food note: The $360 food budget requires strict grocery discipline — packed breakfasts and lunches every day. One tourist-area restaurant dinner can run $40–50/person and will break this category. Food trucks and Bend's food-cart pods are the best value dinners on the route.
Important Notes
Pace: ~32–34 hours of driving total. Significantly more comfortable if both travelers share driving. Trade off every 2 hours.
Mt. Rainier — Grand Park 2 Fire: Sunrise and White River Campground are closed. Plan uses Paradise. Recheck NPS.gov 48 hours before Sep 7.
Mt. Hood fires: Active closures and extreme fire danger in parts of the forest. Trillium Lake and Timberline are the core plan. Verify access on TripCheck Oregon before Day 5.
Crater Lake: East Rim construction = no complete Rim Drive loop. Cleetwood Cove closed all of 2026. West Rim and North Entrance open and excellent.
Yosemite: No timed entry required in 2026. Arrive early anyway — valley parking fills. Waterfalls are very low in September; valley views are still world-class.
Fern Canyon rental car: Confirm with rental company that unpaved road + stream crossings are permitted before booking the vehicle permit. Do not assume.
Fuel discipline: Never below half a tank near Yosemite, Redwoods, Crater Lake, or Rainier. Cell service is unreliable — download offline maps before each park.
Weather & layers: Yosemite afternoons can hit 90°F; Redwood/Cascade mornings can be 40–50°F. Pack warm layers even for a "summer" trip. Sleeping bags should be 3-season rated if using tent backup sites.
