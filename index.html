Crater Lake → Smith Rock (optional) → Bend

🚗 ~205 mi · 4–4.5 hrs · Deepest Lake in the US

ℹ️
Crater Lake 2026: East Rim construction = no complete Rim Drive loop. Cleetwood Cove and all lake access/swimming CLOSED all 2026. West Rim and North Entrance are fully open. The lake is still absolutely stunning from the rim — 1,943 ft deep, intense sapphire blue.
🚗
Depart Grants Pass — 6:00 AM
Route: OR-62 east and south entrance to Crater Lake. Pack a full picnic — park dining is very limited and expensive.
6:00 AM departure
~2 hrs via OR-62 · Enter via south / Annie Spring entrance
🌋
Rim Village + Sinnott Memorial Overlook — 8:15 AM
First panoramic views of the caldera. Crater Lake formed ~7,700 years ago when Mt. Mazama erupted and collapsed. The water is 1,943 feet deep — deepest in North America. The blue is impossible to photograph accurately — it is more intense in person.
8:15 AM45 min⭐ Main Stop
🧭 Rim Village
Drive West Rim Road

Watchman Peak Trail — 9:15 AM
Moderate 1.6-mile hike, best elevated lake viewpoint on the accessible West Rim. 300 ft gain. You see the full caldera, Wizard Island, and on clear days Mt. Shasta and Mt. McLoughlin. One of the best panoramas on the entire route.

Mt. Hood → Timberline → Columbia Gorge → Tenino

🚗 ~275 mi · 5.5–6 hrs · Alpine + Waterfalls

🔥
Check before departure: Mt. Hood has active fire closures. Verify on TripCheck Oregon + InciWeb morning of Day 5. Choose OR-35 vs US-26 based on fire conditions. Multnomah Falls permit doesn't guarantee parking — I-84 shuttle from Rooster Rock is backup.
🚗
Depart Bend — 5:30 AM
Calm morning = best reflection conditions at Trillium Lake. Route to Trillium: US-97 N → US-26 W. Download TripCheck Oregon for live fire/closure alerts.
5:30 AM departure
~2 hrs to Trillium Lake via US-97 N → US-26 W
🪞
Trillium Lake — 7:30 AM
Classic Mt. Hood reflection in the lake — the most photographed view of the volcano. Arrive early to beat crowds and get still water for reflections. $10 day-use fee. Calm mornings only — wind destroys the reflection.
7:30 AM1–1.5 hrs$10 day useDon't Miss
🧭 Trillium Lake
20 min up to Timberline Lodge
🏔️
Timberline Lodge — 9:15 AM
Built 1937 by FDR's WPA — 500 unemployed craftspeople built this by hand in 15 months. The exterior is the Overlook Hotel from The Shining. At 6,000 ft on an active volcano. Every inch hand-carved, hand-forged, hand-woven. Breakfast at Ram's Head Bar (huckleberry pancakes are worth it). Walk the Timberline Trail to treeline for glacier views.
9:15 AM1.5 hrsFree entry · Breakfast ~$13⭐ Main Stop
🧭 Navigate
~1.5 hrs toward Columbia River Gorge · Check fire closure status before choosing route
💧
Multnomah Falls + Benson Bridge — 1:00 PM
620-foot two-tiered waterfall, most visited natural site in the Pacific Northwest. The 1914 Benson Bridge spans the gorge between both tiers — stand in the spray. Fed by perennial underground springs, not surface runoff, so it runs year-round at full force.

Mt. Rainier Paradise → Seattle

🚗 ~195 mi · 4–4.5 hrs (+Labor Day traffic) · LABOR DAY

🚨
Labor Day warning — do not underestimate this. Busiest day of the year at Rainier. Paradise parking fills by 8–9 AM. Reaching Nisqually Entrance before 7:00 AM is mandatory, not optional. Sunrise and White River closed (Grand Park 2 Fire). I-5 into Seattle is heavy from 2 PM onward.
🌙
Depart Tenino — 4:45 AM
Dark departure — headlamps needed. Pack everything the night before: coffee, breakfast, lunch. Route: Hwy 507 → Hwy 702 → Hwy 706 → Nisqually entrance.
4:45 AM — non-negotiable
~1.25 hrs to Nisqually entrance
💧
Longmire + Christine/Narada Falls — 6:45 AM
Historic 1920s NPS district. Roadside photography on the climb toward Paradise. Narada Falls: 168-foot fan waterfall over basalt — morning mist creates rainbows. 5-minute walk from road.
6:45 AM45 min
🧭 Longmire 🧭 Narada Falls
10 min up
🪞
Reflection Lakes — 8:00 AM
Perfect mirror image of Mt. Rainier. The most reproduced Rainier photograph is taken from this exact spot. Only visit if the mountain is clear — skip if clouded in. Subalpine wildflowers (paintbrush, aster, lupine) likely still blooming in September.
8:00 AM25 minMust arrive before 9 AM for still waterDon't Miss
🧭 Navigate
10 min to Paradise
🏔️
Paradise — 8:45 AM to 11:00 AM
5,400 feet on the flanks of a 14,411-ft active volcano. Alpine meadows, glacier views, volcanic geology all at once. Myrtle Falls loop (1 mi, 30 min) — waterfall + glacier view. Nisqually Vista loop (1.2 mi, 45 min) — overlooks the retreating Nisqually Glacier. Don't attempt snowfields — stay on trails.
8:45–11:00 AM~2 hrsATB pass (saves $30)⭐ Final Highlight
